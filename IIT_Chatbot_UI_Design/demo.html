College is more than just courses. It's exploring new interests, joining a club, and finding your passion. It's advocating for your beliefs, raising awareness for a cause, and growing into a leader. It's fun, it's challenging, and it's all about creating memories and learning lessons that you'll carry with you beyond graduation.

Whether you're into railroads, esports, CrossFit, or dance, we have something for you.

Let us help you find your place in the Illinois Tech community. Explore the Office of Student Life.
